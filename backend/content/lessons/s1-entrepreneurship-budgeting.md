## Needs and wants

- **Needs** are things you must have to live and study: food, shelter, school fees, basic clothing.
- **Wants** are things that are nice to have: a new phone, snacks, fashionable shoes.

Money is limited, so we must make **choices**. The thing you give up when you choose something else is the **opportunity cost**.

## What is a budget?

A **budget** is a plan for how you will use your money over a period of time (a week, a month, a term).

$$\text{Income} - \text{Expenses} = \text{Savings (or deficit)}$$

## A sample monthly student budget

| Item | Type | Amount (FRW) |

| Pocket money from family | income | 10 000 |
| Selling avocados at weekends | income | 4 000 |

| Exercise books and pens | need | 2 500 |
| Airtime/data | want | 2 000 |
| Snacks | want | 1 500 |
| **Total expenses** | | **10 000** |
| **Savings** | | **4 000** |

## Steps to make a budget

1. List all your **income**.
2. List your **expenses**, starting with needs.
3. Decide how much to **save first** — pay yourself first!
4. Cut wants if expenses are higher than income.
5. Track what you really spend and adjust next month.

## Why save?

- For emergencies.
- To reach goals (a bicycle, starting a small business).
- Savings can become **capital** to start a business.

Ways to save in Rwanda include a savings account at a bank or SACCO, mobile money savings, and community savings groups (*ibimina*).
